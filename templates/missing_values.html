Section 2 · The "Why" matters

What Type of Missing is It?

The type of missingness determines which fix to use. Click each scenario to explore — wrong choice = biased model.

🎲
MCAR
Missing Completely at Random
Pure chance. No pattern. Any row equally likely to be missing.
Easiest to handle ✓
🔗
MAR
Missing at Random
Related to other observed columns. Predictable from data.
Use KNN / MICE ⚠
⚠️
MNAR
Missing Not at Random
Related to the missing value itself. Needs domain knowledge.
Hardest — beware ✗
