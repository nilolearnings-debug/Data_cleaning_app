Section 1 · Understand the shape

Three Types of Skewness 🎮

Click each type to load a real-world dataset and see its histogram. Notice how the tail pulls the mean away from the median.

📈
Positive Skew
skewness > 0 · tail → right
Mean > Median. Most values are small, rare large values pull the tail right.
Example: Income, house prices
Fix: log, sqrt, Box-Cox
〰️
Symmetric
skewness ≈ 0 · bell curve
Mean ≈ Median. Most ML algorithms assume this shape — the ideal.
Example: Heights, IQ scores
No fix needed ✓
📉
Negative Skew
skewness < 0 · tail → left
Mean < Median. Most values are large, rare small values pull the tail left.
Example: Age at retirement
Fix: reflect + log, square
