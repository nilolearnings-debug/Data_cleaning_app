IQR Method
Robust · No normality needed
Q1 = 25th percentile
Q3 = 75th percentile
IQR = Q3 − Q1
Lower = Q1 − 1.5 × IQR
Upper = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

Best for skewed data or unknown distributions. Extreme values don't affect the bounds themselves.

📐
Z-Score Method
Assumes normal distribution
z = (x − mean) / std
Outlier if |z| > 3

Lower = mean − 3 × std
Upper = mean + 3 × std

Best for normally distributed data. Sensitive to the outliers it's trying to detect — less robust than IQR.
