Section 2 · Choose the right method

Scaling Methods Playground 🎮

Enter comma-separated numbers or use a preset. Click each method to see how it transforms the values with an animated bar chart.

⚖️ Min-Max
x' = (x−min)/(max−min)
Scales to [0,1]. Sensitive to outliers.
📐 Standardization
x' = (x−μ)/σ
Mean=0, Std=1. Better for outliers.
🛡️ Robust Scaling
x' = (x−median)/IQR
Uses IQR — ignores outliers.
